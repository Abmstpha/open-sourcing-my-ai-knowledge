# Open Sourcing My AI Knowledge

I am revisiting the AI notebooks I have collected over the years and sharing them as a series of learning journeys.

Each notebook explores a concept through code, explains the context, and interprets the results. Alongside the notebooks, I will share spontaneous videos walking through the experiments and what I learned.

The journeys follow a technical order, starting with the foundations and building toward more advanced applications.

| Order | Journey | Concepts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Classical Machine Learning | Features, classifiers, dimensionality reduction, and evaluation |
| 02 | Deep Learning | Neural networks, optimization, regularization, and transfer learning |
| 03 | Computer Vision | Image classification, detection, and segmentation |
| 04 | Natural Language Processing | Embeddings, attention, language models, translation, and text evaluation |
| 05 | Speech and Audio | Audio representations and speech-related tasks |
| 06 | Time Series and Forecasting | Temporal patterns, forecasting, and evaluation |
| 07 | Recommender Systems | Recommendation methods and ranking |
| 08 | Reinforcement Learning | Policies, rewards, and sequential decisions |
| 09 | Generative Images and Video | Generative models, diffusion, and visual synthesis |
| 10 | Mistral AI Foundations | Prompting, embeddings, retrieval, tools, and agents |
| 11 | Language Model Adaptation | Fine-tuning and adapting language models |
| 12 | RAG, Tools, and Agents | Retrieval, tool use, orchestration, and MCP |
| 13 | Efficient AI and TinyML | Compression, quantization, and resource trade-offs |
| 14 | Explainable AI | Model explanations and their interpretation |
| 15 | Responsible AI and Privacy | Fairness, data protection, and disclosure risk |
| 16 | Model Deployment and Serving | Turning trained models into usable services |
| 17 | Agricultural AI Applications | Applying learning methods to agricultural problems |
| 18 | Data and Document Automation | Working with structured data and documents |
